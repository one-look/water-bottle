The Department of Management Studies conduct MBA Programme (2 Year Full Time Programme) which is approved by AICTE, New Delhi & Affiliated to Bharathidasan University, Tiruchirappalli. BBA Programme (3 years Under Graduate Degree Programme) is also conducted with affiliation to Bharathidasan University, Tiruchirappalli. The curriculum has exclusively designed as per the industry requirements and to cater the needs of the competitive business world.
Pass with 50% (45% for SC/ST) in any degree from any recognized University is eligible to apply. Prospective candidates have to go through a merit-based admission process. Student Admission to the institute is based on the candidate's performance in Tamilnadu Common Entrance Test (TANCET) or Common Entrance Test (CET) which is conducted by Association of Self financing Arts, Science & Management Colleges of Tamil Nadu and any other Management Entrance Test.

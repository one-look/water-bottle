The Internal Complaints Committee
The Internal Complaints Committee has been formed at Nehru memorial College (Autonomous), to address the issues under Prevention, Prohibition and Redressal of Sexual Harassment of Women Employees and Students. The committee is formed to provide protection against sexual harassment of female employees and students in the Campus and for the prevention and redressal of complaints of sexual harassment and for matters connected therewith or incidental thereto.
Objectives
The objectives of the Internal Complaints Committee of Nehru Memorial College are
- To deal with issues of "Gender Based Violence"
- To conduct "Gender Sensitization Programme"

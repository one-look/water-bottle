Our College has an exclusive research block which houses Research Guides rooms for utilizing it as a space for discussion with research scholars. The Research Block is a four-storey building and consists of 29 air-conditioned and Wi-Fi enabled research rooms.
Each of these rooms is equipped with necessary furniture for research advisor and 3 research students to work simultaneously. These Rooms are provided with desktop Computer and internet facility and cupboard for storing books and research materials and so on. This facility is utilized for interaction/discussion between research supervisor and research scholars/fellow researchers/colleagues on academic and research matters. These rooms provide a congenial atmosphere for the creative/innovative ideas for undertaking research. Undoubtedly, the research block provides the faculty a peaceful and comfortable place for their academic and research pursuits.

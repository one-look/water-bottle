NMC was started with the aim of providing quality education to the uneducated, downtrodden rural people in and around Trichy, at an affordable cost. Our college has an excellent infrastructure with well-equipped laboratories, Seminar hall, ICT Hall, Digital Library and a Well-built auditorium along with Well-maintained Hostels. We provide Quality education to all classes of people through dedicated faculty members and we also try to accomplish excellence in knowledge & application of skills to meet the demands of the scenario. We inspire students to learn and to develop; intellectually, physically and emotionally. We promote innovation and research in various areas of basic, life, computer Sciences and humanities by way of interfacing with various funding organizations, universities and other institutions.

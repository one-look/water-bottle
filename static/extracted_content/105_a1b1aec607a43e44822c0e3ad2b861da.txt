Artificial Intelligence and Machine Learning
Vision and Mission
Vision:
Vision: “To be a premier department that shapes future leaders in Artificial Intelligence and Machine Learning, creating innovative solutions to complex problems while promoting ethical practices, lifelong learning, and holistic development for global impact.”
Mission:
Analytical and Creative Problem Solving: To nurture students' ability to think analytically, creatively, and critically in developing robust, scalable, and maintainable technological solutions for both simple and complex challenges in AI and ML.
Foundation in Core Disciplines: To provide a strong foundation in Mathematical, Scientific, and Engineering principles, enabling students to analyze, design, and solve engineering problems through advanced AI and ML methods in line with modern computing practices.
Technical Proficiency in AI/ML: To impart deep technical competence in AI and ML theories and principles, enabling students to analyze requirements, develop specifications, and design engineering solutions using state-of-the-art technologies.
Collaborative Learning and Teamwork: To foster a collaborative learning environment and team spirit through multidisciplinary projects and professional activities, preparing students to work effectively in diverse, real-world settings.
Ethical Responsibility: To cultivate a deep sense of ethics and integrity in students, ensuring they adhere to industry standards and organizational codes of conduct while developing AI and ML solutions that are socially responsible.
Holistic Development and Lifelong Learning: To equip students with strong technical knowledge, soft skills, and a commitment to continuous learning, empowering them to contribute ethically and sustainably to the emerging field of AI/ML.
Industry-Relevant Education: To align the curriculum with cutting-edge industry trends, ensuring that students are well-prepared for careers in AI and ML, with ample opportunities for internships, research, and placement support in collaboration with leading industry partners.
Global Perspective: To foster a global outlook in students, preparing them to tackle AI and ML challenges on an international scale and contribute to technological progress that transcends geographical boundaries.

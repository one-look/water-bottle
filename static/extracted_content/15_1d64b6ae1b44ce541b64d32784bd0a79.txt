Nehru Memorial College is a progressive educational institution that is dedicated to the pursuit of Education. We have introduced various UG, PG, M.Phil and Ph.D programmes along with other diploma and Certificate courses. We have a team of qualified, experienced faculty members who are very dedicated and hardworking in imparting their knowledge to the younger generations. We prepare and empower students to develop their knowledge, skills and abilities needed to face the ever-changing world. We provide individual attention to each and every student and also provide accessibility to latest trends and modern equipments paving way for new innovations.
I assure the younger generation that Nehru Memorial College will provide you theoretical knowledge along with innovative and creative practical skills and entrepreneurial attitude to achieve remarkable success in your life.

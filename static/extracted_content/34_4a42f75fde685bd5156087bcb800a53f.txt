About the Society
Nehru Memorial College (Autonomous) is an Arts and Science College founded by Late Shri. Mookka Pillai, a great philanthropist whose selfless spirit prompted him to take up the mission and vision of spreading higher education to rural students. It is affiliated to Bharathidasan University, established in 1967, and it is an "A" grade NAAC accredited. The management runs the college under "The Nehru Memorial College Committee (A society registered under TamilNadu societies registration act 1975 with the registration number 33 of 1967)".
